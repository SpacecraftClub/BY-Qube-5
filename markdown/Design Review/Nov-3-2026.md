## Power
- Verify max battery current draw
- Include Margins in the budget
    - Margins account for and take care of non-idealities of the real world
    - 30-40% initially
    - 10-15% as the system becomes better defined
- Inconsistencies in the battery capacity in the flow diagram (420mAh) vs the power budget (300mAh)
- The cold will decrease your capacity and require extra power consumption to run heaters.
    - Internal heating should be considered for true spacecraft
- Make a "Lean"
    - If we end up with more budget, have a plan of improved circuitry to take advantage of this\
        - Example: Maximum Power Point Controllers
- It sounds like you need a Maximum Power Point controller for each panel individually.
- Don't Let the budget overconstrain a key measurement such as power

## Processing
- Use resistors for the defrosting of the camera
- Hydrophobic covering will not prevent frost build up
- Check radio power budget, it is not consistent with radio and it does not need to be included in the processing power budget
- GPS antenna can dangle out in the current design because we are not space worthy yet.
- 17GHz is for clouds, so do not plan on getting ground moisture data.

## Radio
- Add a Balun to match the 50 ohms to the 25 ohms
- Look at elevation for the flight path?
- Have a plan for if the weather baloon does not move laterally like is initially assumed/planned
- Potentially have a second antenna to cover null spots of the first antenna.
- Make sure polarization of yagi antenna matches the polarization of the monopole
    - Safety glasses so the user doen't poke their eye out
- Launch window may be snowy still, this should be considered when selecting a launch date
